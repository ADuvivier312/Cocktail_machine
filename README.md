I carried out this project with five other university students throughout the entire academic year. We had the opportunity to choose the subject of our project; taking inspiration from another cocktail machine, we decided to take on the challenge.

Every part of the development is detailed in the report: risk mitigation, time management, the structural design, the code, and the PCB development.
